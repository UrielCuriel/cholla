# Cholla Acceptance

Reconstruct the work and product context independently. Do not rely on the implementer's summary as evidence. Confirm the current profile is authorized for acceptance and the actor/session did not implement the work; an in-session subagent is not independent.

Map every criterion to an observable command, CI check, test, artifact or inspection. Verify applicable invariants, contracts, integration, reproducibility and regressions proportionally to risk. Record exact evidence links or commands and their result.

Accept only when all required criteria pass and no unresolved blocker/decision remains. Otherwise reject or block with failed criteria, evidence and required action. Never change normative architecture to make acceptance pass.
